Knorm

Most methods in this catalogue need at least one attention weight to decide what matters. Knorm doesn't — it scores each cached key by nothing but the key vector's own length, and evicts the ones that are unusually long.

A shortcut hiding in the key vectors

Devoto et al. measured the attention scores cached KV pairs actually receive during decoding, alongside the L2 norm of their key vectors, across several models and layers. The correlation was strong and consistent: keys with a low L2 norm tend to receive high attention later on, and high-norm keys tend to be mostly ignored. That's a strange thing to be true — a key's norm is fixed the moment it's computed, before any query has looked at it, with no dependence on the current decoding step or anything attention-related at all.

Knorm turns that correlation directly into a score: score = -‖k‖₂. Since kvpress keeps the highest-scoring tokens during compression, a low norm produces a less negative (i.e. higher) score, so low-norm keys survive and high-norm keys are evicted first. There's no forward-looking attention matrix, no running accumulation, no observation window — just a norm, computed once per cached key.

Where this shines, and where it doesn't

Knorm is about as cheap as content-aware compression gets: the key vectors already exist as a byproduct of the model doing its normal work, so measuring their length adds no extra forward passes, no held-out queries, and no per-step bookkeeping — useful when the scoring itself has to be almost free. The tradeoff is that it's a purely correlational shortcut — norm predicts attention on average, but it can't tell "predictably important" apart from "surprisingly important right now" the way a method that actually looks at attention can. In practice its accuracy tends to fall further behind attention-aware methods as the compression ratio gets more aggressive.

Real implementation

kvpress implements this as KnormPress, whose score() is literally -keys.norm(dim=-1). See the source: knorm_press.py. Original paper: Devoto, Zhao, Scardapane, Minervini, "A Simple and Effective L2 Norm-Based Strategy for KV Cache Compression," 2024.
